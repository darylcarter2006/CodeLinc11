We will use DIME method for calculating death benefit
D - Debts
I - Income × years (years = until youngest dependent is self-sufficient)
M - Mortgage
E - Education × number of children

Coverage needed = (Income × max(years_until_retirement, 18 - age_of_youngest_dependent))
                + Mortgage balance
                + All other debts
                + (Education cost per child × number of children)
                + Final expenses (if applicable)

Coverage gap    = max(0,
                    Coverage needed
                    - Existing life insurance
                    - (Spouse income × max(years_until_retirement, 18 - age_of_youngest_dependent))
                    - Liquid savings and assets
                  )

Coverage gap is amount more needed to get to a good death benefit.
Coverage needed is the death benefit our policy owner requires.
Questions to Determine Policy Type

Coverage duration
Do you want coverage for a specific period (e.g. until kids are grown, mortgage is paid off) or for your entire life?
Specific period → Term
Entire life → Perm

Budget
Are you looking for the most coverage for the lowest monthly cost?
Yes → Term
Willing to pay more for added benefits → Perm

Cash value
Do you want to build cash value you can access while you're alive?
Yes → Perm
No → Term

Wealth transfer
Do you want to leave money to heirs beyond just covering immediate needs?
Yes → Perm
No → Term
Tax efficiency

Simplicity
Do you want a simpler policy?
Yes → Term
No → Perm
